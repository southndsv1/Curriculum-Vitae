About Me

I am a Research Associate at Texas State University with a strong background in computational sciences, multiphysics simulations, and artificial intelligence. My research focuses on developing advanced numerical methods for large-scale high-performance computing applications, integrating machine learning with finite element analysis, and optimizing complex engineering systems.

With a PhD in Mechanical Engineering from Virginia Tech and extensive postdoctoral experience at Oak Ridge National Laboratory and the University of Alabama, I bring expertise in domain decomposition methods, bio-inspired design, and scalable HPC techniques. My work spans diverse applications from aerospace structures to advanced manufacturing and genomic data analysis.
